Which platform is your game for?

Players need to know this before they click Play.

Mobile only

Built for touch screens. Best played on a phone or tablet.

PC only

Built for keyboard, mouse or a gamepad. Best played on a computer.

Both

Works well with touch and with computer controls.

Some game makers and apps create games only for phones. They can still open on a computer, but they are harder to play: the touch controls don't connect with the keyboard or mouse, so buttons and gestures may not respond well. Tell us honestly which devices your game works best on, and we will label it so players know what to expect.

How it works

  1. You send the detailsMessage us on Instagram with the six items above.
  2. We review itWe check that the link works and the info is complete. If something is missing, we will ask.
  3. We publish itYour game goes on our games page with its cover, description, category, platform label and a short how-to-play guide.
